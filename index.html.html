About

Twenty-one years as the voice of a destination.

  • 40years in communications
  • 21years leading tourism for Orange County
  • 3towns, one story

For more than two decades, Laurie Paolicelli led tourism for Orange County, North Carolina, as Director of Tourism with the Chapel Hill/Orange County Visitors Bureau. In that role she shaped how the world sees Chapel Hill, Carrboro and Hillsborough: their restaurants and farms, galleries and music venues, historic streets and the people who give them life.

Along the way she became the county's storyteller. Her long-running Orange Slices column profiled chefs, artists, makers, nonprofits and neighbors, turning local character into stories readers sought out and shared.

After 40 years of working and 21 years in the director's chair, Laurie has stepped into a new chapter. Laurie Paolicelli Media carries that storytelling tradition forward, offering writing and communications support to the organizations, businesses and causes that make this region special.
